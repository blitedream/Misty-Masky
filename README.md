# Misty-Masky
0k Team's product for GGJ 2026  

在世界的边缘，有一个被永恒迷雾笼罩的小村庄“安隅镇”。村庄四周的迷雾并非寻常水汽，而是一种古老的障壁——它并非无法穿越，而是会折射人们对世界的认知。村民们代代生活于此，认为迷雾之外空无一物。  
只有一种物品能让人在迷雾中保持方向：面具。  
每个面具都是一种“认知蒙版”，它不改变世界，而是改变佩戴者“看见”世界的方式。有些面具让隐藏的道路显现，有些让危险的深渊隐形，有些则可以有重力反向的能力。  
主角与核心设定  
主角：名叫“启”的12岁男孩，天生对迷雾之外充满好奇。  
启的奶奶在他生日时赠予他第一张“素白面具”，自此他能看见迷雾中第一条若隐若现的小径  
游戏类型:3d动作平台游戏，展现形式是2.5d的样子  
游戏玩法:玩家需要通过已经所获得的面具，不同的面具有不同的功能和作用，然后通过面具的机制来进行通关，向迷雾的世界外面走去。  
At the edge of the world lies Anyu Town, a small village shrouded in an eternal mist. This mist enshrouding the village is no ordinary vapor, but an ancient barrier—not one that cannot be crossed, but one that refracts the way people perceive the world. Generations of villagers have lived here, believing there is nothing at all beyond the mist.  
Only one thing can keep a person oriented within the mist: masks.  
Each mask is a kind of Cognitive Mask. It does not alter the world itself, but changes how the wearer sees it. Some masks reveal hidden paths, some render perilous abysses invisible, and others grant the power to reverse gravity.  
Protagonist & Core Setting  
Protagonist: Qi, a 12-year-old boy born with an unquenchable curiosity about the world beyond the mist.For his birthday, Qi’s grandmother gifted him his first Plain White Mask—and from that day on, he could see the first faint path winding through the mist.  
Game Genre: 3D action platformer, presented in a 2.5D style.Gameplay: Players must utilize the masks they have unlocked, each with unique abilities and functions. Progress through the game by mastering the mechanics of these masks, and journey step by step toward the world outside the mist.
